# GEO Audit Report: Letsuan Design

**Audit Date:** 2026-10-10
**URL:** https://letsuan.com
**Business Type:** Agency / Services (exhibition design, interior construction, visual design)
**Pages Analyzed:** 50 (sitemap, homepage/hubs first; robots.txt `/search` and `/share-widget` respected)
**Method:** Direct HTTP crawl + manual checks, run inline. The skill's 5 parallel subagents were not used. Brand presence used 3 standard web searches (US-only search tool), so brand findings are a lower bound.

---

## Executive Summary

**Overall GEO Score: 57/100 (Poor, but close to Fair)**

The site has a solid technical and structured-data foundation: every audited page returns 200, AI crawler policy is deliberate, llms.txt is present, alt text is complete, and schema is on every page. The main gap is **external brand authority**: none of the three brand searches surfaced Letsuan on Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, or Wikipedia. Content depth is good on project pages but weak on a few short pages, and FAQ-style answer blocks exist on only 6 pages.

## Critical Issues (Fix Immediately)

None. No AI crawler is blocked by mistake, no key page is noindexed, no 5xx errors, and structured data is present on every page.

## High Priority Issues

1. **No external brand footprint found** (Brand Authority). Searches for "Letsuan Design" with Taiwan exhibition design, the Chinese name 麗荃室內裝修, and reddit/youtube/linkedin returned no Letsuan pages. AI systems rely on third-party mentions for entity recognition and citation, so this is the largest score drag.
   - Fix: publish case studies on third-party sites that AI systems index (see CLAUDE.md backlog: Behance, ArchDaily, Designboom, award entries), and set up a LinkedIn company page with project posts.

## Medium Priority Issues

2. **Titles run long.** 38 of 50 pages have `<title>` over 60 characters. Search results truncate long titles, and the new zh-TW and EN titles (rewritten 2026-10-10) are long by design. Consider trimming the brand suffix or the second segment on the top ~10 traffic pages.
3. **Descriptions run long.** 17 pages have meta descriptions over 160 characters.
4. **Thin pages for citability.** 10 pages have under 300 words of visible text. The thinnest: `project-fullchamp-brochure` (76 words), `project-just-only-environment` (94), `project-led-kinetic` (139), `project-securities-office` (143).
5. **FAQ answer blocks on only 6 of 50 pages.** FAQPage schema is present on those 6, so the fix is to add FAQ blocks to service pages and the top project pages, not to add schema.
6. **Homepage response time.** Median TTFB across the sample is 0.36 s, but the max is 2.25 s. Check which page is the outlier before optimizing.

## Low Priority Issues

7. **Blog or resource content is absent.** The site has no article-style pages (only 2 pages carry NewsArticle schema), so there is little topical content for AI systems to cite on process or industry questions.
8. **llms-full.txt and llms.txt are not linked from the homepage** (informational; both are served correctly).

## Category Deep Dives

### AI Citability (62/100)
- Word count per page: median 659, range 76 to 1,804.
- 10 of 50 pages are under 300 words (see issue 4).
- Project pages have long-form narrative sections (strategy, scope, outcomes), which is a strength for passage-level citation.
- Weak spot: few pages open a section with a direct, quotable answer (for example, "Letsuan Design is a Taiwan-based exhibition design studio with ..."). The llms.txt summary does this well; the body copy largely does not.

### Brand Authority (25/100)
- Searches found no Letsuan pages on Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, or Wikipedia.
- The search tool is US-only and standard mode, so some Taiwan-specific profiles may not appear. Confirm manually on LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and the Taiwan business registry before weighting this heavily.
- Score is low because the measurable signal (external mentions) is near zero.

### Content E-E-A-T (60/100)
- Experience and expertise signals are present: 42+ projects since 2010, named clients (national museums, Ørsted, HTC, Shogakukan), an interior decorator registration certificate (`EducationalOccupationalCredential`), and a named legal entity.
- Missing: named individual authors or designers on project pages, and a team or founder page with credentials visible in the body (not just in the schema).

### Technical GEO (85/100)
- All 50 pages return HTTP 200. Median TTFB 0.36 s.
- Content is server-rendered: homepage headings and body copy are present in the initial HTML.
- AI crawler policy: training crawlers blocked (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended); search and user crawlers allowed (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot).
- llms.txt (8.9 KB) and llms-full.txt (38 KB) present.
- Canonical present on all 50 pages. hreflang present on all 50 (en, zh-TW, x-default).
- Inline font CSS from the 2026-10-01 audit is no longer in the homepage (now 2 `@font-face` rules plus Google Fonts link).
- Images: 496 total across the sample, 0 missing alt text.

### Schema & Structured Data (80/100)
- Every audited page carries JSON-LD. Types found across the sample: Service, Offer, OfferCatalog, WebSite, ProfessionalService, GeneralContractor, ContactPoint, PostalAddress, OpeningHoursSpecification, EducationalOccupationalCredential, BreadcrumbList (42 pages), ExhibitionEvent (30), CreativeWork (37), ImageObject (38), FAQPage (6), NewsArticle (2), VideoObject (2).
- `GeoCoordinates` appears on all pages. Per your standing decision (memory: no map coordinates for studios), this is left as is.
- Opportunity: FAQPage on service pages (Exhibition Design, Interior Design, Visual Design, Construction), using the Q&A already present in the body copy.

### Platform Optimization (40/100)
- Positive: llms.txt present; bilingual (EN and zh-TW) with correct hreflang, which helps Google AI Overviews and Bing Copilot target the right language.
- Not measured directly: Bing Webmaster and Google Search Console AI-feature reports (Search Console performance report is available for the account and could be checked for AI-surface impressions).
- Score is low because no platform-specific profile (YouTube channel, Reddit presence, Wikipedia entry) exists.
