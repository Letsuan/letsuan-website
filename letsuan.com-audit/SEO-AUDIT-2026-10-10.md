# SEO / AEO / GEO Audit: letsuan.com (SEO-AEO-GEO Ultimate, seo-audit lane)

Date: 2026-10-10
Scope: homepage, What We Do, About, Portfolio, and project pages, EN and zh-TW (50-page sample from sitemap, robots-allowed)

## 2. Findings by lane

### 2.1 Technical (seo-technical)

**Confirmed, healthy**
- All 50 sampled pages return 200. Median TTFB 0.12 s (max 0.39 s) in the latest run; the earlier run measured 0.36 s, so timing varies.
- Canonical present on 50/50. hreflang attributes present on 50/50. Reciprocity of hreflang pairs was not verified.
- Content is server-rendered (headings and body text are in the initial HTML).
- Googlebot-style fetch of all 88 sitemap URLs returns 200.

**Confirmed, needs action**
- 3 legacy Blogger comment-feed URLs were reported as 403 in Search Console (validation started 2026-08-28). They now return 301 to live pages. Google has not re-crawled them yet. Owner: Leo (Search Console revalidation click).
- 4 URLs return 404 (`www.letsuan.com/default`, `cdn-cgi/l/email-protection`, a malformed `…htmlsearch?q={search_term_string}` link, and one other). None is linked from the repo; they come from old or external links. Owner: Leo to decide whether to leave as 404 (correct) or add redirects in Cloudflare.
- 2 label pages blocked by robots (`/search/label/...`). This is intentional under the current robots rule. No action unless the policy changes.

**Unresolved**
- The index report shows 105 indexed pages, but the sitemap lists 88. The 17-page gap has not been identified. Owner: assistant to list the indexed URLs from Search Console (requires the 105-item drill-down) before any conclusion.

### 2.2 Page content and metadata (seo-content, seo-page)

**Confirmed**
- Median visible word count across the sample: 659 (range 76 to 1,804).
- 4 project pages under 150 words: Fullchamp Catalog (76), Just Only (94), LED Kinetic (139), Securities Office (143). Two other short pages (zh-TW contact, zh-TW privacy policy) are short by design.
- `<title>` longer than 60 characters: 38 of 50 pages. These are the rewritten 2026-10-10 titles. The effect on click-through is still being measured (baseline in memory; re-check after about 2026-10-20).
- Meta description longer than 160 characters: 17 of 50.
- Every page has an H1 and no images are missing alt text (496 images checked).

**Fixed during this work (verified in the deployed HTML)**
- "EST. 2010" label changed to "PRACTICING SINCE 2010" on About and What We Do (EN and zh-TW). The company registration year is 2014; "12+ YEARS" is correct as written.

**Supported opportunities (not yet implemented)**

- Trim the longest titles only after the CTR baseline comparison (decision owner: Leo, after ~2026-10-20).

## 更新：17 頁差距已查清

Search Console 的已收錄清單共 105 頁，全部讀取後的結果：

- **18 頁是舊 Blogger 網址**（`www.letsuan.com` 或 `tw.letsuan.com`），不在 sitemap 裡。線上都會轉址到現在的頁面，例如 `www.letsuan.com/2022/05/amazing-homeland-exhibition.html` 會轉到 `letsuan.com/...`。這些網址會隨著 Google 重新檢索而逐步消失，不需要人工處理。
- **87 頁是網站本身的網址**，sitemap 有 88 頁，所以只差 1 頁。這一頁還沒有查出是哪一頁，影響很小。

結論：差距主要來自舊 Blogger 網址，不是網站結構的問題。

sitemap 的 88 頁中，只有一頁未出現在已收錄清單：`https://letsuan.com/zh-TW/project-orsted-climate-expo`（2024 台灣氣候行動博覽會 沃旭展館，中文版）。

## 更新：結構化資料與頁面文字比對

抽樣 12 頁（首頁、服務、關於、作品集、聯絡各中英文，以及 Universe 專案頁中英文），逐一比對 JSON-LD 的文字欄位與可見頁面文字。

- **作品名稱**：結構化資料與頁面 H1 意思一致，只有標點或措辭略有不同（例如「Universe — 500 Pieces LED Kinetic Light」與「Universe 500 Pieces of LED Kinetic Light」）。不影響正確性，不需要修改。
- **中文頁面的服務名稱與專長**：已改為中文，與頁面一致。
- **機構基本資料**：電子郵件、地址、別名、服務語言等，沒有以完全相同的字串出現在頁面上，但都是真實資料。建議確認頁面的聯絡資訊區塊與結構化資料一致。
- **機構描述**：中文頁面的 `description` 是摘要，不是頁面上的原句。這是正常的做法。

結論：沒有發現需要修正的錯誤。唯一可選的是讓聯絡資訊的呈現格式與結構化資料一致，屬於低優先的整理工作。
