Securities Company Office A Contemporary, High-End Workplace

CLIENT

Securities Company

LOCATION

New Taipei City, Taiwan

SIZE

200 ㎡

CATEGORY

Interior Design, Construction

Located in the Banqiao District of New Taipei City, this new office was designed around a contemporary, high-end feel. Quality finishes carry that tone throughout, including a feature curved wall and custom-made handles at the entrance. The office now houses a dedicated waiting area, a kitchen and breakout area, a boardroom, executive offices, and an open-plan workstation area. Construction was handled by Le Tsuan's in-house team.
