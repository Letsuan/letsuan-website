Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide — name, email, phone number, company, budget range, and project details. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics beyond what is needed to operate the contact form.

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry and discuss a potential project. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.
