Company Profile

Letsuan Design is a licensed design company based in Taichung and Taipei, Taiwan, delivering museum-grade exhibitions and commercial interiors since 2014. Our work spans museums, aquariums, cultural venues, corporate spaces, and public installations: any physical space built to communicate something is within our field.

The team moves comfortably between narrative design, spatial planning, visual systems, and on-site construction, keeping every detail anchored to a single core concept.

Team composition scales with each project, bringing in licensed architects, fire safety engineers, civil engineers, quality-control staff, and multimedia programmers as individual project needs require.

ESG / SUSTAINABILITY

Sustainability
as a System

We define sustainability as the systematic practice of ESG, not the choice of an eco-friendly finish.

In the renovation industry, sustainability is often reduced to a green-building certification sticker. That falls well short of what we consider adequate. Sustainability has to be a baseline standard built into every design decision, material choice, and construction step, not an optional upgrade at extra cost. It comes down to whether a space can be used for the long term, whether the people on site are kept safe, and whether the client's interests are protected by a documented process.

01 ENVIRONMENTAL

Built for the long-term health of the site

For heritage buildings, historic structures, and older houses, construction convenience is never the priority. Once the fabric is damaged, it can't be undone, so we put the long-term health of the site and its structure first.

First Zero-Damage Structural Systems

we build on a zero-damage structural principle. Every design starts with an as-built survey and structural inventory, then uses reversible methods, non-invasive fixings, distributed loading, and zoned protection to avoid irreversible damage to existing beams, columns, brick walls, flooring, and historic surfaces. Every fixing point has to be fully removable.

Second Green Materials & Energy Efficiency

we treat Taiwan's regulatory minimum of 60% green-material content as a floor, not a target, and keep raising that ratio. We favor low-formaldehyde, low-VOC, recyclable materials sourced from local production, and add high-efficiency LED lighting with circuit control, variable-frequency air conditioning, reworked daylighting and ventilation paths, and detailed thermal and air-tightness work, so the energy savings show up in daily use.

Third Reuse & Waste Reduction

from assessing salvageable materials for reuse to sorting construction waste and controlling dust and noise on site, we calculate lifecycle cost rather than one-off material savings. The goal is a balance between environmental responsibility and client cost that holds up over time.

Social Social

One safety standard, no exceptions for subcontractors

Whether a job site is truly sustainable comes down to how it treats its most vulnerable workers. We hold ourselves equally responsible for on-site occupational safety and for the labor conditions of our subcontractors.

As a licensed interior decoration business, the regulatory and safety standards we require of subcontractors match what we require of our own team, with no distinction between the two.

That standard runs through daily practice: pre-shift briefings and toolbox talks, fall and electrical-shock protection, dust and noise controls, permits and supervision for hot work and work at height, and training and qualification checks for contractors. We require reasonable working hours, full labor and health insurance and pension contributions, and zero wage withholding or illegal employment. That commitment extends to every worker on site.

In addition Project Insurance

every large-scale project carries additional project insurance, including contractor's all-risk insurance, employer's liability insurance, and third-party liability insurance. The intent is to make sure clients, users, and the on-site team are fully covered before an incident happens, not after.

Governance Governance

Compliance as a process you can trace

Most disputes in the renovation industry come down to something that was never made clear up front. We make that our responsibility to settle before work begins.

First Transparent Disclosure

we proactively disclose our interior decoration business registration number, company registration details, and professional staff qualifications, and clients can verify any of it at any time.

Second Licensed Permit Filing

we handle professional renovation permit applications and review submissions on the client's behalf. That covers drawing production, structural safety, fire escape routes, accessibility, and partition-wall review, through to filing and on-site inspection, so the design intent stays realized within a legal framework throughout.

Third Traceable Documentation

from application through inspection, every compliance procedure, material certificate, construction record, inspection photo, and warranty document is kept complete with cloud backup and stays traceable. Contracts, change orders, and warranty scope are all set out in writing, so every commitment leaves a record that can be checked.

To us, sustainability is risk management, not a cost.

It is respect for the site, protection for the people on it, and accountability to the process.

Get all three right, done by the same hands, and design finally has a chance to last.

FAQ

What does Letsuan Design specialize in?

Letsuan Design is a licensed design company based in Taichung and Taipei, Taiwan, specializing in museum-grade exhibitions, commercial interiors, visual identity systems, and design-build contracting.

When was Letsuan Design founded?

The company was founded in 2014 and has completed more than 42 projects for museums, government agencies, and international brands.

Does Letsuan Design handle both design and construction?

Yes. Projects go from design to construction without changing hands: the same team stays on from the first sketch through final installation.

Is Letsuan Design a licensed interior decoration business?

Yes. We hold interior decoration business registration number 40E2007328 and handle professional renovation permit applications and review submissions on our clients' behalf.
