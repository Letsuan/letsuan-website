EST. 2014 — CONTINUOUS PRACTICE

Why insist on integrated design-build?
Because drawings are imagination, and the site is the experience.

A hairsbreadth of misalignment in a lighting angle, a sliver of a gap between a case and the wall: each is a hole in the story. Drawings never make it to the site. Only one team, staying on it end to end, can keep that story intact once it's built.

Plenty of exhibitions look beautiful on paper and lose something once they're built. It's usually because curation, design, and construction sit with different vendors. Responsibility gets diffused, and the gap that's left is the one the visitor has to live with.

Letsuan Design chose the heavier, more accountable path: the same team stays responsible from the curatorial framework through the final lighting walkthrough before opening. Woodwork, metalwork, lighting, and multimedia are all fabricated and calibrated on site by that same group.

When the same people are watching over both the drawings and the site, the story doesn't get lost on the construction floor, and the exhibition delivers the experience it promised.

Feasibility

Before a single line is drawn, we complete the site survey, review code and structural constraints, and confirm client requirements, mapping site conditions and the budget ceiling up front so the design phase never runs into a scheme that turns out to be unbuildable.

Design & Engineering

Design and structural engineering run in parallel, so the concept is buildable within site and code constraints from day one. Architectural, structural, and MEP drawings are cross-checked together, cutting the risk of conflicts surfacing during construction and sending drawings back to the drawing board.

Fabrication & Testing

We fabricate everything in-house, testing mechanical, lighting, and AV systems before anything leaves the workshop. Every unit goes through trial assembly and quality checks before shipping, so problems get solved in the workshop rather than on site.

On-site Installation

We install trade by trade on schedule under our own site management, coordinating with venue operations and any third-party inspectors. Progress and quality are managed in step, so drawings and the built result stay consistent at every handoff between trades.

Handover

We close with a final walkthrough, documentation, and sign-off with the client, completing any licensor or regulatory procedures along the way and leaving behind a complete maintenance manual and a point of contact, so there's someone to call for day-to-day operations after opening.
