THE STUDIO

Le Tsuan Design is an interior, exhibition and visual design studio based in Taiwan. Our team moves fluidly between narrative design, spatial planning, graphic systems and on-site construction — staying involved from the first sketch to the final install, and scaling in licensed architects and engineers as each site demands.
