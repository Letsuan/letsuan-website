THE STUDIO

Le Tsuan Design is an interior, exhibition and visual design studio based in Taiwan. We work across museums, aquariums, cultural venues, corporate spaces and public installations, wherever a physical space needs to carry a message.

Our team moves fluidly between narrative design, spatial planning, graphic systems and on-site construction, which lets us stay involved from the first sketch to the final install, keeping every detail in service of the same idea.

We scale the team to the project, bringing in licensed architects, fire-safety engineers, civil engineers and quality-control inspectors as each site and regulatory requirement demands.

The project never changes hands between design and construction.

SUSTAINABILITY

Sustainability runs through how we build: non-invasive structural systems that protect heritage sites and historic fabric.

Our green building material usage rate meets the current legal minimum of 60%, balancing environmental responsibility with client cost.

As a licensed interior decorator, we apply the same regulatory and safety standards to our subcontractors that we apply to ourselves.
