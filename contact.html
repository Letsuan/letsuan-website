FAQ

SERVICES & SCOPE

What services does Le Tsuan Design provide?

We provide end-to-end exhibition, interior, and visual design — from curatorial concept and spatial planning through graphic design, fabrication, and on-site construction. Most projects are delivered as a single point of contact from concept to installation.

What types of clients and projects do you work with?

We work primarily with museums, government agencies, and corporate clients on exhibitions, permanent galleries, brand environments, and public installations — as well as smaller-scale interior and graphic design projects.

Do you work outside Taiwan?

Yes. While most of our work is based in Taiwan, we've delivered projects internationally, including exhibitions in Spain.

HOW WE WORK

How do you start a new project?

Every project begins with understanding the site, the audience, and the client's goals, followed by concept development, then detailed design and engineering before construction begins.

Do you handle construction and fabrication, or only design?

We handle both. Our in-house team manages design development through construction, fabrication, and installation, so the same team that designs the space also builds it.

Do you provide 3D visualizations or concept renderings before construction?

Yes. Every project moves through concept renderings and 3D visualizations before fabrication begins, so clients can review and approve the design intent ahead of construction.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary by scope — a small fitment project can take one to two months, while a large-scale museum exhibition with custom fabrication typically takes six months to a year from concept to opening.

TERMS & PRACTICALITIES

What is the typical project budget range?

We don't publish set pricing — every project is scoped and quoted individually based on size, materials, and construction complexity. Get in touch with your project details for an estimate.

Do you sign NDAs or protect client confidentiality?

Yes. We routinely sign NDAs for government and corporate clients, and treat all project materials — briefs, budgets, and unreleased content — as confidential throughout and after the engagement.

Do you provide maintenance after an exhibition or installation is completed?

Yes. Maintenance is included during the warranty period, and we continue to offer paid maintenance support on a case-by-case basis after the warranty expires.
